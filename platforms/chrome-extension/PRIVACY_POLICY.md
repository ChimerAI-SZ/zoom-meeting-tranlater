# Privacy Policy for BabelAI Chrome Extension

**Last Updated**: January 1, 2025

For the complete Privacy Policy covering both the BabelAI macOS application and Chrome Extension, please visit:

**https://babelai.app/privacy.html**

## Quick Summary

BabelAI Chrome Extension:
- Only captures audio when you click "Start Current Tab"
- Stores configuration and transcript history locally in Chrome storage
- Does NOT track your browsing history
- Does NOT sell or share your data
- Complies with Chrome Web Store policies and GDPR/CCPA
